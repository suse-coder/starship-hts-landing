**E17–E30 Guidance rebuilt on physical actuator limits (catch still open → F1).**

- Lift-direction dynamics are physical in predictor and command: the lift is carried with v̂ by
  the passive trim, and only the rotation ABOUT v̂ is actuated (cold-gas yaw ≈ 0.005 rad/s²,

- Removed a non-physical rule (G0): a "belly must face down" lift floor. The flapless hull is
  axisymmetric and bare steel all round, so any roll about v̂ is equivalent.
- Belly flop: the same σ law while the descent is shallow; a ground-fixed lift-azimuth law once
  steeper than −60° (σ's reference spins there). Before the flip, the nose turns to the nearest
  N/E/S/W toward the pad and the roll is set so the pins face the east/west rails after the flip.
  The flip's ≈ 80 m/s kick along the nose then starts the divert.

- Result: delivery ≈ 1.7–1.8 km from the tower at the flip; the landing burn reaches the catch
  point with the pins aligned but runs out of propellant or loses the final approach. Not yet caught.
